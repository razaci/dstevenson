NOT JUST A JOB.
A WAY OF LIFE.

David Stevenson knows the world of big machines, long days, and work that doesn't wait for perfect conditions. Today, he's working in the oil fields. His trucking equipment is parked in a yard in Idaho — but the story behind those rigs is anything but parked.

This is a tribute to the drive, determination, and backbone of a man who knows what it means to work hard and keep going. No invented awards. No made-up company history. Just the kind of respect that doesn't need exaggeration.
